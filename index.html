1. Introduction

Welcome to VoiceStudio AI ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data and audio creations. This Privacy Policy explains how VoiceStudio AI collects, processes, stores, and safeguards information when you use our Android mobile application.

This policy is designed to comply with the Google Play Developer Program Policies (including User Data, Permissions, and Data Safety requirements), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

2.1 Microphone & Audio Recordings

VoiceStudio AI requires access to your device microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO) to provide its core functionality:

  • Studio Voiceover Recording & Multi-Track Editing: When you record voiceovers in the Record tab or edit clips in the Editor tab, your audio files (WAV/M4A) and waveform metadata are stored strictly locally on your device inside the app's private sandboxed directory. We do not upload your local voiceover recordings or edited mixes to any external server.
  • AI Voice Live Conversations: When you voluntarily tap "Start Live Call" in the AI Voice tab, your live microphone audio stream is transmitted securely over encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS) to Google Firebase AI Logic (Gemini Live API) solely to generate real-time spoken AI responses and live session transcriptions.

2.2 User-Imported Media Files

When you load a reference backing track (audio or video) or import an external audio file into the multi-track editor, the app uses the zero-permission Android System File Picker (Storage Access Framework). VoiceStudio AI only accesses the specific file you select and does not scan or read other files on your device.

2.3 Advertising & Device Identifiers (Google AdMob)

VoiceStudio AI displays interstitial advertisements via the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To serve, measure, and prevent fraud in advertisements, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • Device and Advertising Identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AAID).
  • Coarse IP-based Location & Device Diagnostics (device model, OS version, screen dimensions, network connection type).
  • Ad Interaction Events (ad impressions, clicks, and dismissal events).

You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

2.4 App Integrity & Security (Firebase App Check)

We use Google Firebase App Check (and reCAPTCHA Enterprise) to verify that requests to the Gemini AI service originate from an authentic instance of VoiceStudio AI and to prevent unauthorized API abuse.

3. Android Permissions Used

Permission Purpose in VoiceStudio AI
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Captures microphone audio for studio voiceover recording and real-time AI voice conversations. Requested at runtime only when you initiate recording or a Live AI call.
android.permission.INTERNET Required to connect to Firebase AI Logic (Gemini Live API) for AI voice conversations and to load Google AdMob advertisements.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checks network availability before loading advertisements or establishing AI voice sessions.
android.permission.VIBRATE Provides subtle tactile feedback on studio transport controls.

5. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

  • Local Recordings & Projects: All recorded voiceovers, multi-track timeline projects, and studio preferences are stored locally on your device. You can delete individual recordings at any time from the Library tab, clear AI conversation transcripts using the Clear button in the AI Voice tab, or remove all app data by clearing storage or uninstalling the application.
  • No Developer Cloud Database: We do not maintain a custom developer server that stores your personal profile, contact details, or recorded audio files.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or audio recordings. Audio files are only shared with other applications (such as WhatsApp, YouTube, or system storage) when you explicitly tap the Share button on a recording and choose a destination app via the Android Share Sheet (FileProvider).

7. Children's Privacy

VoiceStudio AI is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can assist you promptly.

8. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

  • Revoke microphone permissions at any time in Android Settings > Apps > VoiceStudio > Permissions.
  • Delete any or all locally stored voice recordings and transcripts directly inside the app.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising or reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
